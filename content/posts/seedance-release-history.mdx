Seedance release-history searches often mix an official launch date with a provider rollout date, a model ID date, and a regional availability date. This timeline separates them so a release claim stays attached to the right source.

Dates and capabilities can change in product documentation. When a workflow depends on a model version, record the source and date you checked.

## Seedance timeline

| Seedance 1.0     | Earlier ByteDance Seed video model release    | Established the family and the original text-to-video direction                               |

The timeline describes release context. It does not mean every provider offers every version or feature on the same day.

## Seedance 2.0 release date and what changed

The official Seedance 2.0 launch described a unified multimodal audio-video architecture. It supports combinations of text, image, video, and audio inputs on supported surfaces, plus video editing and extension workflows.

A Seedance 2.0 release-date search may also be asking when the model became available on Dreamina, BytePlus, Higgsfield, or another platform. That is a separate rollout question. Use the provider’s own model page for access, price, and settings.

The current Seadance Video browser generator is built around available Seedance 2.0 workflows. See [Seedance 2.0](/seedance-2-0) for its current text-to-video, image-to-video, and video-to-video path.

## Seedance 2.5 release and current access

ByteDance Seed introduced Seedance 2.5 on July 31, 2026. Its official announcement describes:

- up to 30 seconds per generation with multi-round extensions;
- broader multimodal reference inputs;
- more precise editing, including timestamp direction;
- green-screen and camera perspective workflows;
- clay-render or white-model control for spatial blocking.

BytePlus later published Seedance 2.5 access and API materials for supported markets. Consumer and API availability should still be checked separately because a model can appear in one product before another.

For current access questions, use [Seedance 2.5](/seedance-2-5). For release-date and availability updates, see the [Seedance 2.5 release guide](/blog/seedance-2-5-release-date-and-overview).

## How to track a version correctly

When a provider page says “Seedance,” capture:

1. the full friendly model name;
2. the versioned model ID, if available;
3. provider and region;
4. product or API surface;
5. input mode and output settings;
6. plan and price source;
7. date checked.

This prevents a 2026 model result from being compared with a 2025 demo, or a consumer app feature from being presented as a public API feature.

## Major change categories

### Duration

Seedance 2.5 is positioned for 30-second storytelling and extensions. Older versions and tiers may expose shorter clips.

### References

Seedance 2.0 already introduced broad multimodal inputs. Seedance 2.5 expands the reference workflow and adds stronger editing and blocking claims.

### Editing

Compare whether the provider supports video-to-video, extension, timestamp-level direction, green screen, camera perspective, and reference-based edits.

### Resolution and price

Do not copy resolution or pricing from one version into another. Check the exact model, mode, plan, and returned file.

### Rights and moderation

Every release and provider can apply different rules to faces, likeness, copyrighted characters, logos, music, and source footage.
