## Introduction

Welcome to Seadance Video, an AI video generation platform available at [seadance.video](https://seadance.video). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you access the site, create an account, purchase credits, or submit content for video generation.

## Information We Collect

1. **Account Information**
   - Name, email address, authentication details, and profile preferences.
   - Used to authenticate users, provide account features, and deliver customer support.

2. **Generation Inputs and Outputs**
   - Text prompts, reference images, reference video URLs, generation settings, and generated results.
   - Used to process generation requests, display task history, enforce safety requirements, and improve service reliability.

3. **Usage and Device Information**
   - Feature interactions, timestamps, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, IP-derived location, and diagnostic logs.
   - Used for analytics, abuse prevention, security, and product improvement.

4. **Payment Information**
   - Billing details and transaction records required to process purchases and subscriptions.
   - Payment card data is handled by our payment providers and is not stored directly by Seadance Video.

5. **Cookies and Similar Technologies**
   - Used to maintain sessions, remember preferences, measure site performance, and support security controls.

## Third-Party Processing

Generation requests may be processed by third-party AI, storage, moderation, analytics, authentication, email, and payment providers. These providers receive only the information required to perform their services and process it under their own privacy terms.

## Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Generated media and task records may be retained for account history, download, moderation, support, and operational purposes. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information:

- To service providers that operate the platform.
- To comply with law, legal process, or valid government requests.
- To investigate fraud, abuse, security incidents, or violations of our terms.
- As part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of business assets.

## Your Choices

You may update account information through your settings and request account or data deletion by contacting us. Some records may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or financial compliance purposes.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service, providers, or legal requirements change. The revised policy becomes effective when posted on this page.
